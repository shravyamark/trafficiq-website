Privacy and security

Sensitive video deserves a smaller trust boundary.

TrafficIQ places privacy-sensitive filtering inside confidential compute. Raw imagery is decrypted only inside an approved, attested workload, then minimized before downstream analytics and data sharing.

  • Application-layer encryption from camera to TEE
  • Attestation-gated key release
  • Face and plate redaction before broader use
  • Policy-controlled buffering, retention, and deletion
  • Role-based access, logging, and auditable handoffs
